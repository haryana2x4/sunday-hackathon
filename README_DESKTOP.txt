-----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO RUN IN 1-CLICK FROM YOUR LAPTOP
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Simply double-click the file on your Desktop:
   >> START_SUNDAY.bat
Or inside this folder:
   >> run.bat

This will automatically:
1. Start the high-performance local server on port 8765.
2. Launch your browser immediately to http://localhost:8765/
3. Enable all connected devices (phones, tablets, laptops) to connect.

2. HOW TO SHOWCASE TO FRIENDS & JUDGES (ANY DEVICE)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
A. ON THE SAME WI-FI / LOCAL NETWORK (PHONES, TABLETS, LAPTOPS):
   Tell your friend to connect to your Wi-Fi and open this URL on their phone:
   >> http://192.168.1.6:8765/

   Or open the Project Showcase page:
   >> http://192.168.1.6:8765/project

B. ANYWHERE IN THE WORLD OVER THE PUBLIC INTERNET:
   Open Command Prompt / PowerShell and run:
   >> npx localtunnel --port 8765

   You will get an instant HTTPS link (like https://huge-mice-visit.loca.lt)
   that ANYONE in the world can open on their iPhone, Android, or PC!
